# Anxiety by Academic Year — Bivariate Grouped Bar Chart

## Description
This grouped bar chart shows the distribution of anxiety across the four years of university study. For each academic year, two bars are displayed: students without anxiety (yellow) and students with anxiety (blue).

## Insight
At what point in the career is anxiety most concentrated? This visualization reveals that the first years of study concentrate the largest number of students in the sample, which naturally also implies a greater number of cases of anxiety. However, when looking at the proportion of students with anxiety within each year, it is evident that the condition does not disappear with career advancement. This concentration of data in the first years also coincides with the period of greatest adaptation to university life. However, it's also where there are more students overall, so it's important to interpret this with caution. This data is relevant for designing early interventions aimed at new students.
