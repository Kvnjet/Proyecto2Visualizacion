# Depression Distribution — Univariate Bar Chart

## Description
This chart shows the distribution of depression among university students in the sample. Each bar represents the count of students who reported having depression ("Yes") versus those who did not ("No").

## Insight
The "No" category (students who did not report depression) represents 65.3% of the total. The "Yes" category (students who reported depression) makes up 34.7%. Approximately one in three students surveyed presents symptoms of depression, which indicates a considerable prevalence within the university population studied.
