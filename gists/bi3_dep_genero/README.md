# Depression by Gender — Bivariate 100% Stacked Bar Chart

## Description
This 100% stacked bar chart compares the proportion of students with and without depression between female and male students. By normalizing to 100%, the chart allows a fair comparison between gender groups regardless of their different sample sizes.

## Insight
Female students show a higher proportion of reported depression than their male counterparts. While this may partly reflect a greater willingness among women to recognize and report symptoms, it signals that gender is a relevant variable that should not be ignored when designing university mental health policies.
