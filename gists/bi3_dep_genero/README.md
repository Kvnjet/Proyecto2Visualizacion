# Depression by Gender — Bivariate 100% Stacked Bar Chart

## Description
This 100% stacked bar chart compares the proportion of students with and without depression between female and male students. By normalizing to 100%, the chart allows a fair comparison between gender groups regardless of their different sample sizes.

## Insight
The graph shows that female students have a higher proportion of depression than their male peers. This difference, although it may be influenced by the greater willingness of women to report symptoms, indicates that gender is a variable that cannot be ignored in the design of university mental health policies.
