# CGPA Distribution — Univariate Bar Chart

## Description
This chart displays the distribution of students across five GPA ranges (CGPA: Cumulative Grade Point Average). Each bar represents the number of students falling within a specific academic performance range, from 0–1.99 up to 3.50–4.00.

## Insight
The vast majority of students surveyed are concentrated in the CGPA range between 3.00 and 3.49, suggesting that the sample is primarily composed of students with good or very good academic performance. However, there is a notable presence of students in lower ranges, which will be relevant when exploring the relationship between performance and mental health.
