# Mental Health Conditions by Gender — Faceted 100% Stacked Bar Chart

## Description
This faceted chart displays the prevalence of three mental health conditions — depression, anxiety, and panic attacks — broken down by gender. Each facet (Female / Male) shows a 100% stacked bar for each condition, enabling direct cross-gender comparison.

## Insight
This faceted visualization is especially revealing: in virtually all conditions, women show higher percentages. It is striking that anxiety is the most prevalent condition in both genders, even surpassing depression. Panic attacks, although less frequent, are also present in no small proportions.
